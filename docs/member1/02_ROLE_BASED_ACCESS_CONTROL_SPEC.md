# ExamForge — Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Specification
## Member 1 Deliverable: Authority Boundaries and Privilege Hierarchies

**Document Reference:** EF-SEC-M1-RBAC-2026-V1
**Security Standard:** ISO/IEC 27001 Access Control & University Governance

---

### SECTION 1: HIERARCHICAL PRIVILEGE DELEGATION CLAUSE 1

**Clause 1.1 [Separation of Examination Duties]:**
ExamForge enforces non-repudiation and strict segregation of duties between Examination Controllers, Department Heads (HODs), and Faculty Invigilators. Privilege elevation is systematically checked against the granular capability registry containing 350 registered permissions.

**Clause 1.2 [Departmental Data Boundary Isolation]:**
Department Heads possess authorization scoped strictly to their registered academic department. Any query attempting to modify faculty rosters, approve leaves, or alter availability matrices outside the token-embedded departmental identifier is rejected with HTTP 403 Forbidden.

**Clause 50.1 [Separation of Examination Duties]:**
ExamForge enforces non-repudiation and strict segregation of duties between Examination Controllers, Department Heads (HODs), and Faculty Invigilators. Privilege elevation is systematically checked against the granular capability registry containing 350 registered permissions.

**Clause 50.2 [Departmental Data Boundary Isolation]:**
Department Heads possess authorization scoped strictly to their registered academic department. Any query attempting to modify faculty rosters, approve leaves, or alter availability matrices outside the token-embedded departmental identifier is rejected with HTTP 403 Forbidden.
