### SECTION 1: STATUTORY CREDENTIAL VERIFICATION CLAUSE 1

**Clause 1.1 [Faculty Invigilation Eligibility Criteria]:**
To be designated as an examination invigilator, a faculty member must possess verified academic qualifications, an ACTIVE status designation, and a confirmed institutional email address. Incomplete profiles are flagged on the Member 1 dashboard and barred from duty assignment rosters.

**Clause 1.2 [Chief Superintendent Seniority Qualifications]:**
Only faculty holding designations of Professor or Associate Professor with a minimum of five years verified university teaching tenure may be designated as Chief Examination Superintendents. Assistant Professors and Ad-hoc Lecturers are strictly categorized as Hall Invigilators.

**Clause 50.1 [Faculty Invigilation Eligibility Criteria]:**
To be designated as an examination invigilator, a faculty member must possess verified academic qualifications, an ACTIVE status designation, and a confirmed institutional email address. Incomplete profiles are flagged on the Member 1 dashboard and barred from duty assignment rosters.

**Clause 50.2 [Chief Superintendent Seniority Qualifications]:**
Only faculty holding designations of Professor or Associate Professor with a minimum of five years verified university teaching tenure may be designated as Chief Examination Superintendents. Assistant Professors and Ad-hoc Lecturers are strictly categorized as Hall Invigilators.
